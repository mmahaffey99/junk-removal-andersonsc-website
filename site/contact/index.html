Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be home for the junk removal?

In many cases, yes — especially on your first job with us. However, if the items are outside or in an accessible location and you've given us clear instructions, we can sometimes work without you present. We'll discuss this when we confirm your appointment.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, check, and most major credit cards. Payment is collected after the job is complete and you've confirmed you're satisfied.

Do you charge extra for large items?

We price based on volume and weight — not by the item. The estimate we give you before we start is the price you pay.
