Frequently Asked Questions About Appliance Removal in Anderson

Can you remove appliances that are still connected?

In most cases, yes — we can handle the disconnection of electric appliances and those connected to water lines. For gas appliances, we recommend having your gas company or a licensed plumber cap the line before our arrival, which is a standard precaution for everyone's safety. Just let us know when you book and we'll walk you through exactly what you need to prepare.

Do you recycle old appliances?

Yes, whenever possible. Scrap metal from appliances — including the steel drums of washers and dryers, refrigerator compressors, and more — gets recycled rather than sent to a landfill. We're committed to responsible disposal, and recycling metal is both environmentally responsible and often required by law for certain appliances.

I have a refrigerator with freon — is that a problem?

Not for us. We work with certified disposal facilities that handle refrigerant recovery properly. This is actually one of the key reasons to use a professional appliance removal service in Anderson rather than trying to haul it yourself — improper disposal of refrigerants is illegal under the Clean Air Act and carries stiff penalties.

How much does appliance removal cost in Anderson, SC?

Pricing depends on the number of appliances, their size and weight, and how easy they are to access. Appliances in tight spaces or on upper floors may require additional effort. We always give you a free, honest estimate before we begin any work — call (843) 642-8417 to get yours.
