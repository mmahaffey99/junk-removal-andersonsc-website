### What We Do

We haul away the things that are too heavy, too bulky, or too inconvenient to deal with yourself — furniture, appliances, yard waste, construction debris, estate contents, and general household junk.

Our process is straightforward: you call or fill out the form, we give you a free estimate, we show up when we say we will, and we do the work. No hidden charges, no upselling, no crew standing around running up the clock.

### Our Commitment to Honest Service

We don't do anything that would embarrass us if our neighbor watched. That means:

- **Honest pricing** — you get a clear quote before we start. If something changes, we tell you why before we proceed.
- **Showing up on time** — we give you a time window and we hold to it. If something changes, we call ahead.
- **Doing the work ourselves** — we don't hand your job off to a sub or a stranger we found online. Our crew handles your property.
- **Leaving it cleaner** — once the junk is out, we sweep up and take everything we said we would.
