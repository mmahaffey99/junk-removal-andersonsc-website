## NOTES ON KEYWORD STRATEGY

1. **Do not keyword-stuff.** Use primary keywords in H1, meta title, and naturally in the first 100 words. Secondary keywords appear naturally throughout.
2. **Location + service is the formula.** "[service] [city] SC" is the pattern that drives local rankings.
3. **Answer real questions.** The FAQ sections and blog posts are specifically designed to capture "People also ask" rankings on Google.
4. **Links matter too.** Once live, get the site listed on Google Business Profile, Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, and any local Anderson SC directories — each is a citation that helps rankings.
